# Manage Kide.app ticket sales

Open `/admin` and sign in. Under **Website**, open **Site Settings** and find
**Ticket sales (Kide.app)**.

1. Paste your public Kide.app event link into **Kide.app ticket URL**. Use the full
   `https://kide.app/...` address for your ticket sales page.
2. Set **Button text**, or leave the default **Osta liput**. The short navigation
   button always says **Liput** so it fits on mobile. Other links show your button
   text together with **Kide.app**.
3. Choose the locations under **Show links in**:
   - **Navigation (desktop and mobile)**: a ticket button in the desktop menu and
     mobile quick links/menu. On mobile, Hinnasto stays available in the menu.
   - **Homepage hero**: a ticket button below the event date.
   - **Prices page**: a ticket sales panel above the price information.
   - **Programme page**: a ticket sales panel above the programme.
   - **Footer**: a ticket link in the footer of public pages.
4. Check **Show ticket sales links** and click **Save**.

All locations use the same URL. Links open Kide.app in a new tab. Saving refreshes
the website's cached settings and pages; reload the public page to see the change.

To pause sales links or hide them after sales close, uncheck **Show ticket sales
links** and save. Your URL, button text, and placement choices are kept. You can
also remove individual placements or clear all of them to hide every placement.
To change events, replace the URL and save once.

Ticket links start disabled, with no event URL. They stay hidden until enabled
with a valid Kide.app URL. You can prepare the URL and text while links are hidden.
This controls website links only; ticket availability and payments are managed in
Kide.app. Any links you manually add to rich text content are managed separately
in that content.
